# Too Precious to Mine

**By Alexandra Crosby**
*Inside Indonesia, Edition 98 (Oct-Dec 2009)*

## Overview

This article documents Central Java farmers' resistance to Semen Gresik's planned cement factory in South Pati, which would mine 1,432 hectares at Kendeng Mountain's base. The campaign highlights the Sedulur Sikep (Samin) community's environmental and cultural defense of their homeland.

## The Proposed Development

Semen Gresik planned a 2.5-million-ton annual capacity factory affecting eight villages. The factory would extract limestone, threatening the region's 87 natural springs and karst ecosystems.

## Samin Philosophy & History

Named after 19th-century activist Samin Surontika, these communities practice peaceful resistance through "organic farming methods" and "egalitarian use of only Ngoko, the low-level form of Javanese language." They famously "refused to pay taxes to the colonial authority" under Dutch rule.

### Core Practices

- Organic farming methods
- Egalitarian language use (Ngoko Javanese only)
- Peaceful resistance traditions
- Communal land stewardship

## Modern Activism

Young environmental groups merged Samin philosophy with contemporary methods, including:
- Rock and punk music
- Zines
- Facebook groups and blogs
- Culture jamming

Organizations like **SuperSamin** and **anakseribupulau** mobilized resistance.

## Protest Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2005** | Land prospectors arrived under false pretenses |
| **October 2006** | First large-scale protest with 500 motorbikes and cultural gatherings |
| **March 2008** | Environmental action headquarters established |
| **January 2009** | Peaceful road blockade escalated to violence; nine farmers arrested |
| **May-June 2009** | Charges reduced; elder Sutarno died at age 100 |

## Environmental Concerns

Activists warned that "47 springs could be drained completely" by limestone mining operations disrupting delicate underground water systems. The karst landscape is essential for:

- Freshwater springs
- Agricultural irrigation
- Flood prevention
- Biodiversity preservation

## Legal Victory

The Supreme Court ultimately ruled in favor of the community, annulling Semen Gresik's license to build in Sukolilo-Pati (May 2009).
